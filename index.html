What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant bonds with the bone, it supports a custom implant crown, bridge, or denture. Dental implant treatment can be used for a single tooth implant, several missing teeth, or full mouth implants and implant-supported dentures. Your dentist will recommend the option that fits your situation, bone health, and budget.
